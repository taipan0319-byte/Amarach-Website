What we need from you before we set up a new machine

When a new computer is on the way, a little preparation on your side means it arrives at your desk working: signed in, printing, and running your software, instead of arriving as a project.

The list

  1. The old machine, still running. Don’t wipe it, recycle it, or let it get packed away. Files, settings, browser bookmarks, and program licenses migrate far more reliably from a live machine than from a bare drive.
  2. Sign-ins for your key accounts. Microsoft 365 or email account, and passwords for the software you use daily. If a password is lost, tell us early. Recovering one adds a day, discovering that at install time adds a week.
  3. Your business software details. The name of your practice management, accounting, or industry software, and your vendor’s support contact if you have one. Many vertical applications must be installed or licensed through the vendor, and we coordinate that. It just goes faster when we can start the call before the new machine arrives.
  4. The printer and scanner situation. Which ones you use, and how they’re connected. Ten minutes of looking at the current setup saves the classic day-two call: everything works but it won’t print.
  5. What bothered you about the old machine. Slow at a specific task? Screen too small? A program you always meant to get rid of? A new setup is the cheapest moment to fix these.

What you don’t need to do

You don’t need to research specifications, compare processors, or pick a model. Tell us what the machine is for and we’ll spec it to the job. That’s the equipment service. And every machine we deliver gets a full Dave Perfect pass before it reaches your desk, so it arrives clean, current, and quiet.
